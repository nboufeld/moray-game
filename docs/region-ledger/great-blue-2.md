depth 2 — no gateway wing; the inbound connection crosses THE WORLD'S
EDGE itself, off the Drop Plains' far pole. Disc centre r = 940 on
azimuth 5.31 (world ≈ (528.9, −777.2)), radius 220. Seed

## Concept

The Drop Plains ends on a fall into violet; this is the country the
fall was into. Its painted deep promised layered violet ridges beyond
the void — this region is that painting, entered. The diver crosses the
Under-Blue, climbs the FAR WALL out of the dark, and surfaces onto THE
OTHERSHORE — a bare milky saddle, sun again after the violet — and then
the world falls away a SECOND time at THE BRINK, and THE DEEP STEPS
open: a nested amphitheatre of great violet shelves bowed around the
World's Edge itself (every shelf edge is an arc centred on the void the
diver crossed), each shelf one value deeper. The subject is the same as
the whole province's: space itself, and single great events standing in
it. The register inverts the Drop Plains': there the prairie was alive
and the void was the far mystery; here the water is the vast thing and
the one river through it carries all the life.
