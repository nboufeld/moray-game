## Per-item diagnosis and fix

### #8 — lumen-garden

**Interior diagnosis** (`WING-lumen-garden_wpol-before`): the critic's
"near-black murk with three glow dots", confirmed and rooted three ways.
(1) The canonical stand (tierb r3: y −6.5, pitch −0.5 at r 42 on the
axis) stares at the HEART's deliberately-bare floor band — and the
wave-8 beds hug the walls, so almost no glow lives where the frame
looks. (2) Everything unlit measured as murk: the mood strips ~all of
the rig (`sun 0.94 / hemisphere 0.85 / ambient 0.32` removed) and the
T1 frond carpet shipped with NO emissive by an explicit "the dark earns
its darkness" ruling — the verdant-2 re-pass already proved that ruling
fails under a mood this deep ("the mood light multiplies every albedo
down faster than keys can climb"). (3) The kit-scale marks (0.2 m
halos, 0.085 m bulbs) are sub-pixel at the stand's 5–10 m under
densityGain 0.05 fog.

**Interior fix** (three rounds):
- **The dusk lift** (the verdant-2/Blue3Cover device, wing-side, the
  kit palette-pure): the night-frond carpet takes an emissive floor
  0x36506a @ 0.5 (r1's 0x2c4256 @ 0.34 measured as still-murk — the
  first round's lesson re-learned in its own wing); the lantern strands
  0.12 → 0.3; the lantern bulbs grow 0.085 → 0.115 m (same topology).
- **The glow massed into constellations**: authored anchor strings
  through exactly the band the frame holds (near-axis r 45.8–49.2 past
  the heart's far rim, in-band flanks at ≥ 0.064 rad with the 0.5 m
  scatter counted, a gate-side pair for the descending diver), built
  with the wing's own bed recipe — the glowColony kit's bud+halo
  discipline (apex-gradient domes, `emissivemap_fragment` tinted light,
  halos hung just above the tips) at the garden's proven brightness.
  r1 used `buildGlowColony` itself with flank strings at r 40–44 and
  measured INVISIBLE: the stand is AT r 42, so anything beside it is
  out of frame, and the kit's fixed 0.2 m halos vanish under this fog.
  Two draws (`lumen-constellation-beds` + `-halos`), fresh
  `SEEDS.wingLumenGarden ^ 0xb40a` substream, ~90 buds ≈ 7.2k tris.
- **The wave-8 beds' halos** 0.32 → 0.5 m and motes 0.09 → 0.11 m.
- **The pose** (r3): y −6.0, pitch −0.38 — the tierb pose was authored
  to judge the T1 floor and stared at the bare heart; the shallower
  pitch holds the floor constellations AND the midwater bulbs and wall
  beds in one frame. (The nursery/lumen tierb rounds set the precedent
  for pose fixes at the canonical stand.)

**Door diagnosis** (`DOOR-lumen-garden_wpol-before`): the "dark vertical
band" is the gate veil itself — the tierb-r3 "soft shaft" spec
(w 2.0 × h 5.2, sillLift −0.6) makes planes 2.3–3.7 m wide but
5.7–9.9 m tall: a near-black stripe a third as wide as it is tall,
towering over the saddle against the bright backdrop, running out the
top of frame. Not a rendering artifact — a proportion error.

**Door fix** (r1, held through r3): wider than tall (w 2.15 × h 2.6,
sillLift −0.2) so the planes stack as a dusk INSIDE the notch; far
plane corners r ≤ 38.8 < 39, so the heart law holds by construction

planes-only ruling is retired: a 40-count drift of dim cyan motes
(`particulate 0x8fd4e8`) over the sill is the one mark that says
"bioluminescent night" from the bowl — and with the band gone the notch
now frames the Crown Jelly Sovereign hovering in its own motes.
**Verdict r1/r3: the band is dead; the doorway is the premise.**
