## 4. Verdict — to the owner

**The answer is still NOT YET — but for the first time it is CLOSE, and the distance is
now measurable in artifacts, not in absences.** Your bar has two halves. "Beautiful
wherever you look, even on a plain simple road" — the wave largely won that half: the six
roads now speak their provinces, the mid-down axis produces top-twelve frames instead of
two-tone wash, and life moves where the eye travels while the registered rests keep their
silence. "Amaze me, truly and really" — that half is still short, and the tell has moved:
where the first report charged emptiness, this one charges edges. The screen-door stipple
band now sits across two of your best skies; translucent veil quads float hard-edged in
repaired beats; a kelp card cuts a razor triangle across Calamity's opening sky; great-blue
is finally not broken but is still the province you describe rather than the one that
amazes you. Nine of seventeen charges are dead, five more moved honestly, and only one got
worse — the wave was real work, honestly done, and the world is better in almost every
frame I read. Kill the stipple, the veils, and that one kelp card, give great-blue's wall
the conviction its mooring arch already has, and the next critic should be arguing about
which twelve to leave out — not whether the bar is met.
