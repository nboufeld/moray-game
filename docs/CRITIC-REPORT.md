# CRITIC REPORT — Moray Dive Hunt, Whole-World Pass

Independent critique, fresh eyes, adversarial by mandate. Judged against the owner's bar, verbatim:
"a place you can wander in and be amazed, dreamy — amaze me, truly and really" and
"beautiful wherever you look, even on a plain simple road."

Evidence base: 164 frames in `visual-qa/` — all six journey spoke chains (80 frames, tags
`JOURNEY-*`), 64 off-road frames I authored myself (`CRITIC-*`: roadside-cross / mid-down /
floor-up / lost-bearing, four per region across all sixteen regions), and the Tier-B wing set
(20 frames, `WING-*` / `DOOR-*`, ten wings interior + doorway). Capture
scripts: `scripts/journey-shots.mjs` (existing), `scripts/critic-offroad.mjs` (mine),
`scripts/tierb-wing-shots.mjs` (existing).

Protected-stillness areas per MASTER §1.2 were held exempt from "emptiness" findings.

---

## 0. Overall verdict

**The world amazes in moments — it does not yet amaze as a place.**

There are eight or ten frames here that genuinely earn the word "dreamy": pale-3's daybreak
pillars, the golden oasis, the calamity rib-cage reveal, the great-blue mooring at twilight,
verdant-3's canopy from below. Those are the frames the builders authored and polished, and
they are good — some are excellent.

But the owner's bar is "beautiful wherever you look, even on a plain simple road," and that is
exactly where the world fails today. The connective tissue between beats — every pass corridor,
every mid-water down-look, most off-spine bearings — collapses to the same formula: a bare
sand plane, a horizon silhouette band (often with visible card geometry), and a scatter of one
tuft kit. The journey reads as a slideshow of authored postcards separated by minutes of
nothing. Wander 30 degrees off the spine and the amazement stops.

Verdict: **NOT YET.** No single ship-blocker in the render sense, but the gap between beat and
road is a program-level miss against the stated bar. The punch list below is ordered by what
closes that gap fastest.

## 2. Province arc verdicts

### Verdant (regions 1–3) — arc: half lands
The intended crescendo (meadow → vale → grove) only arrives at verdant-3, which is the
best-authored region in the world: mushroom-cap canopy silhouettes, motes, god rays; its
floor-up view (`CRITIC-verdant-line-3-03-floor-up`) is a top-12 frame. But verdant-1's Sunwell
is a soft nothing at its own heart, the vale gradient is flat, and off-spine verdant is a
gradient wash with sprigs (`CRITIC-verdant-line-2-04-lost-bearing`). Life is nearly absent —
the "shoal network" doctrine is not visible in captures.

### Smoking (regions 1–3) — arc: dips between strong bookends
The gorge lip and caldera rim open well; the Choir and ember-dawn close well. Between them:
repeating tan plains with identical dark-shard tufts, and the Comb (F1) flat-walling the
region-2 centrepiece — off-road it is worse: `CRITIC-smoking-marches-2-01-roadside-cross`
catches the Comb as a featureless purple slab filling a third of the frame. The night-threshold
beat is emotionally flat — a darker tan plain, not a threshold. The Choir's bottle chimneys
clone at five sizes in one glance (`CRITIC-smoking-marches-3-01`). But credit where due: the
smoking-1 mid-down (`CRITIC-smoking-marches-1-02`) — ember vents glowing through the haze —
is the best unauthored down-look in the world, and smoking-2's floor-up catches rays circling
the chimney tops. The province has the world's best off-axis moments and its barest plains.

### Pale (regions 1–3) — arc: strongest finish, weakest middle
Daybreak (anvil pillars in mint against teal, rays) and sun's-doorstep (light dome over
standing stones) are two of the best frames in the world. But the road there is the emptiest
in the game — pass23 and the pale-3 threshold are near-identical bare-sand compositions, and
the mother-crown pose clips into an unlit magenta polygon (region-1's landmark, broken at its
own authored pose). Off-road makes it worse: `CRITIC-pale-passage-1-04-lost-bearing` catches a
razor-straight wall-card slab standing on the horizon — the F2 card-face family lives in pale
too — and `-01-roadside-cross` finds a lone banded purple disc sitting on bare sand like a
dropped prop. The one delicate off-road frame is `-03-floor-up`, sky through bone-coral fingers.

### Great-blue (regions 1–3) — arc: DOES NOT LAND
This is the province meant to sell "100x vast," and it reads as empty rather than vast. The
wall — the entire identity of the province — is a flat teal card band at every authored beat
(F2), and it stays a flat band from off-road bearings too (`CRITIC-great-blue-2-01`).
`CRITIC-great-blue-2-02-mid-down` is the emptiest frame of the whole capture set: a pure
indigo wash, edge to edge, nothing in it — "vast" delivered as "absent." Slope-reveal and
gnomon are decent (gnomon's orange mottling is noisy for the register), and two frames
genuinely land: the twilight mooring arch and the daybreak keel silhouette. Odd inversion:
great-blue-1's unauthored frames — the meadow roadside-cross, the grass-slope down-look with
blue coral boulders, the little pink shoal streaming past the floor-up — are better than its
authored slope poses. The meadow is real; the wall is a card.

### Golden (regions 1–3) — arc: warm but interchangeable
The oasis and noon-bell are strong beats (noon-bell has a visible tiled-texture patch on the
plain behind). Everything between is the same tan plain as smoking with a warmer tint; the
saddle-reveal reveals nothing; evening-horizon closes the province on a huge featureless dune
face. The wing-door canyon frame is good — and its gateposts are the same double-lobe boulder
silhouette as verdant, pale, and calamity. Off-road splits the same way: the oasis from above
(`CRITIC-golden-waste-1-02-mid-down`, palm canopies ringed by fish) is one of the best
unauthored frames in the world — and the same frame is crossed corner-to-corner by a
razor-straight water-volume edge, the cleanest single proof of the C4 seam problem. The
golden-1 roadside dune also carries an abrupt purple-to-gold diagonal shading seam. And
`CRITIC-golden-waste-3-04-lost-bearing` is the "plain simple road" test failed verbatim: a
featureless dune face edge to edge, with a thin dark hairline seam kinking across the sand.
The hoodoo trunk close-up (`-2-04`) shows the kit CAN carry a frame — the plains around it
just don't.

### The wings — a coin-flip
Ten Tier-B wings sampled, interior + doorway each. The good ones are genuinely good:
sargassum-sky's floating raft seen from below is a top-12 frame, ruins-terrace earns its
arches and fish, glass-cove and mangrove-roots have real character. The rest are a coin-flip:
current-run is a seagrass corridor ending at a plain tan wall with one flat blue mound for a
horizon; lumen-garden's bioluminescent premise doesn't read (near-black murk); nursery-shallows
is compositionally cramped. And every single wing shows the flat-polygon backdrop problem
somewhere (C4). Wings share the verdant blade-tuft grass wholesale — the kit monotony (C2)
is loudest here.

### Sunken Calamity (single region) — arc: LANDS
Tonally the most coherent stretch of the game: sorrow-gate's drowned statues in haze, the urn
husks of suffocated-mile, the bent mast with pennants at wound-gate, the rib-cage reveal, the
green last-grove. The mournful register holds. Uniquely, it also holds OFF-road: the dead-palm
grove from an unglamorous bearing (`CRITIC-sunken-calamity-1-01`) still composes, the mid-down
actually has content (palm crowns, drifting debris, a dark hull mass), and the floor-up through
bent fronds against the streaked sky (`CRITIC-sunken-calamity-1-03-floor-up`) is a top-12
frame. This is the only province that survives my whole off-road battery — proof the bar is
reachable region-wide, not just at beats. Marred by F3 notches in its two best frames (the
same hard-edged horizon band reappears at the off-road lost-bearing), confetti-like shard
scatter on mid-march slopes, and one more parade of double-lobe sentinels at quiet-rim.

## 3. Cross-cutting findings

**C1 — The pass corridors are one region wearing six tints.** pale-09, golden-05, great-blue-09,
smoking pass frames: identical formula (bare plane + horizon band + tuft scatter). The
connective network is where the player spends the most uncommitted time, and it is the least
authored surface in the game. This is the single largest gap against "beautiful on a plain
simple road."

**C2 — Kit monotony: the double-lobe boulder and the one tuft kit.** The same potato/double-lobe
rock silhouette is the gatepost in golden, the sentinel in calamity's quiet-rim, the roadside
filler in verdant and pale (recolored each time). One blade-tuft kit and one dark-shard tuft
kit carry nearly every plain. Recognizable kit repetition breaks the dream faster than
emptiness does.

**C3 — Unauthored view axes.** Mid-water looking down is a featureless two-tone wash in
nearly every region sampled (`CRITIC-*-02-mid-down`) — no caustic dapple, no shoals below, no
floor pattern. The one exception proves the rule: smoking-1's vent field glows through the
haze from above (`CRITIC-smoking-marches-1-02`) because something was actually built on that
floor. Floor-up works only where a canopy or skyline exists (verdant-3, smoking chimneys).
The world is authored for one camera: horizontal, on the spine.

**C4 — Horizon/water card geometry is visible at authored poses.** F2/F3 are one framework
problem: razor-straight card edges, stepped rectangle corners, two-tone water seams
(`WING-wreck-meadow`), the moonlit-lagoon "moon pool" reading as a flat cyan polygon, hard
teal triangles in the ruins-terrace sky (`WING-ruins-terrace`), and a translucent ghost
pyramid hovering in the current-run doorway (`DOOR-current-run`). Ten of the ten Tier-B wings
sampled show at least one flat-polygon backdrop read. Every occurrence is a hard tonal break
with the soft painted register.

**C5 — Scale does not read as scale.** Vastness is delivered as emptiness (great-blue passes,
golden evening-horizon). The frames that DO read as vast (daybreak keel overhead, noon-bell
towers) do it with a large silhouette anchoring the space — proof the fix is compositional,
not budgetary.

**C6 — Life is scarce.** Across 100+ frames: a handful of fish schools (pale pass23-back,
golden hub, calamity bowl). Doctrine promises shoal networks; captures show still water. The
Ghibli register needs motion and witness — even two or three drifting silhouettes change a
bare plain into a quiet one.

**C7 — Light grammar repetition.** The single vertical god-shaft is the one move used
everywhere (golden saddle, oasis, noon-bell, calamity last-grove, smoking). It works — but it
is becoming the world's only lighting sentence.

## 5. The strongest 12 frames (critic's own pick)

1. `JOURNEY-pale-12-pale3-daybreak` — anvil pillars in mint against teal, rays. The world's best silhouette sentence.
2. `CRITIC-verdant-line-3-03-floor-up` — canopy from below, god rays, warm surface. An unauthored pose that amazes: proof the bar is reachable.
3. `JOURNEY-great-blue-12-great3-daybreak` — dark keel overhead, light shaft, motes. The one frame where great-blue feels vast.
4. `JOURNEY-calamity-07-sunken1-the-reveal` — rib-cage arcs across the horizon. Mournful and huge (fix the notches behind it).
5. `JOURNEY-golden-04-golden1-oasis` — palm-kelp swale in warm golds. Purest Ghibli register in the game.
6. `JOURNEY-great-blue-08-great2-mooring` — ruined arch, twilight purple meadow, sparkle motes.
7. `JOURNEY-pale-13-pale3-suns-doorstep` — light dome over the dune crest and standing stones, lone fish silhouette.
8. `JOURNEY-golden-08-golden2-noon-bell` — three bell towers, shaft, jellyfish overhead.
9. `JOURNEY-calamity-06-sunken1-wound-gate` — bent mast and pennants (the left half; the right half is empty dune).
10. `JOURNEY-calamity-08-sunken1-last-grove` — green kelp and green shaft; hue-shift storytelling that works.
11. `CRITIC-verdant-line-3-01-roadside-cross` — mushroom-cap grove across the road, motes column; off-road done right.
12. `CRITIC-sunken-calamity-1-03-floor-up` — dead palm fronds bowed against a streaked pale sky. Grief, composed, from a pose nobody authored.

(Bumped from the list with regret: `JOURNEY-golden-02-wing-door` — the canyon exit works, but its gateposts are the same double-lobe boulders as four other provinces.)

## 6. To the owner, directly

Your builders can hit your bar — daybreak, the oasis, the rib cage, and one accidental
masterpiece looking straight up through verdant-3's canopy prove it. What they built is a
necklace: twelve real jewels on a string of bare sand. Every "12/12" was scored at the jewels.

The fastest route to "amaze me, truly and really" is not more landmarks. It is: (1) fix the
card geometry the camera keeps catching (the wall, the notches, the moon pool) because one
hard rectangle kills a soft world instantly; (2) give the roads the same love as the beats —
one reveal, one companion shoal, one light change per corridor; (3) author the down-look and
the up-look, because a diver spends half the game looking exactly there; (4) buy two more
silhouettes per kit slot before buying anything else. The dream is close. It breaks between
the postcards.
